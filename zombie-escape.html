Mission

Find the Key (big building, east), the Engine (south-west building), the Wheel (north-west building) and the Fuel (hideout, south centre). Install them on the car in the north-east yard, then drive out of the east gate. Running costs stamina. Zombies only hear you when you run, fight or drive. Night lasts 2 minutes, and twice as many zombies hunt you. Band-aids (🩹) heal you, with a 50 second wait between uses. If any player dies, the game is over.
